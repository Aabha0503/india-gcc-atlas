India GCC Atlas — policy and ecosystem research workspace

These CSVs are a research tracker, not legal or incentive advice. The policy
records below are official-page leads only. The underlying policy clauses have
not been reviewed, so benefits, dates, thresholds, application requirements,
setup-model requirements, and company eligibility are intentionally blank or
marked for verification.

The Telangana policy index and sector overview do not establish the existence
or eligibility of an IT/GCC incentive. No GCC policy document was located in
the policy-index content reviewed.

Eligibility ladder values are deliberately separate from source quality and
company eligibility. A policy must not be advanced automatically. To mark
eligibility assessed, enter reviewer notes and attach a checklist source URL.
To mark eligibility confirmed, record a company-specific written-evidence URL.
The application has no identity system; CSV-editing controls assume deployment-
level access control that restricts the app to authorized reviewers.

service_providers.csv is the shared provider directory for Policies and
